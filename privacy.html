Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how GG Daily Uploader ("the App"), operated by Jannik Harzenetter, Takustraße 95a, 50825 Köln, Germany ("we"), handles information. The App is a private tool used exclusively by its operator to publish their own videos to their own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

1. Who uses the App

The App has a single user: its operator. It is not offered to the public, and no other people can sign in or connect accounts.

2. What data the App accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile information of the operator's own account (e.g. account name, avatar) and the permission to upload and publish videos to that account (scopes such as user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish).
  • YouTube / Google: the permission to upload videos to the operator's own YouTube channel (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) and to read public statistics of that channel.

The App does not access, collect or process data of any other TikTok or YouTube users.

3. How data is used

Access is used solely to publish videos that the operator has created and reviewed, including their title, description and hashtags, and to show the operator basic statistics of their own videos.

4. Storage

Access tokens are stored only locally on the operator's own computer. No data is sent to or stored on servers operated by us. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

5. Third-party services

The App uses the official TikTok and YouTube APIs. Their own privacy policies apply: TikTok Privacy Policy, Google Privacy Policy. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

6. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or in the Google account settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions). Locally stored tokens are deleted when the App is removed.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
